Job Application Tracker

Your token and data stay in this browser. The extension only talks to Notion.

1. Connect Notion

  1. Go to notion.so/profile/integrations and create a new internal integration.
  2. Copy its secret (starts with ntn_ or secret_) and paste it below.

2. Choose a database

Already have a database? Open it in Notion, choose ••• > Connections, add your integration, then paste its URL.

Or let the extension create one

Make an empty page in Notion, add your integration to it under ••• > Connections, and paste the page URL. The extension adds a "Job Applications" database with all the columns.
